# Mouse artwork

The runtime artwork now comes from the user's actual [MX4 model](models/mxmaster4mac-whitesilver-ar-360.glb). Nine transparent768×768 renders, their hashes, camera settings, finite wheel timeline and registered surface anchors are in `src/mx4_linux/assets/mouse/`. See [model preparation](model-bake.md) and [model inventory](model-inventory.json). GTK consumes the baked images; the Three/browser tools used for preparation are not runtime dependencies. Actual capabilities, battery and channel status come from device discovery. The supplied application logo remains unchanged.

The source model has no animation tracks. The bounded wheel movement is newly authored illustration, using the actual wheel geometry and materials. Its static power/channel LEDs do not establish live state. Reduced motion uses the static overview. No full wheel spin or unverified thumb-wheel rotation is shipped.

Before the model was supplied, a provisional silver mouse illustration was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Its unused project copy was removed in favor of the actual model; the original generated result remains in the image tool's output directory. Its generation prompt is retained below for provenance only.

Generation prompt:

> Create one production-ready transparent-background product illustration asset for a native desktop mouse settings application. A photorealistic original 3D studio render of a silver/light gray ergonomic premium productivity mouse in the visual family of an MX Master4: right-handed sculpted body, high arched palm rest, a broad curved thumb rest on the LEFT side, finely ribbed concentric/horizontal grooves across the left lower haptic thumb panel, two slim side buttons and a small metallic horizontal thumb scroll wheel above that panel, a ridged metallic main scroll wheel between the upper buttons, a small mode-switch button just behind the main wheel, a tiny green battery indicator. Show the whole mouse from an elevated three-quarter angle, mostly top-down, with the front/nose pointing toward the upper-left and the palm rest/tail toward lower-right. The left thumb side and its controls must be clearly visible. Clean silver shell, precise seams, restrained realistic materials, fine soft contact shadow only, no surface or backdrop. Orthographic-style product view with minimal perspective distortion; subject centered with generous transparent padding, tall composition. Crisp details at native screen size of about280px tall, high resolution source. Absolutely no text, letters, numerals, logos, trademarks, cables, UI, labels, arrows, circles or other objects. Transparent alpha background, no white or checkerboard background. This is original product artwork for a control-panel UI, not a screenshot or a graphic poster.

The user-supplied model and its rendered derivatives are used for this local rebuild; no embedded redistribution license is asserted. The screenshots remain reference evidence in `img/`.
